seva

a personal planning assistant

seva is a small assistant i built for myself. it helps me plan my days around my goals, reminds me what's next over telegram, and checks in on how things went.

it's used only by me. it isn't offered to anyone else, and there's nothing to sign up for.

to plan around my schedule it connects to my google calendar. what it can see and do is set out in the privacy policy.
