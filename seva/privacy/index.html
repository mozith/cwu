privacy

for seva, a personal assistant used only by its maker. last updated 19 october 2026.

what it accesses

  • reading: my google calendars, so it knows when i'm busy.
  • writing: only a separate calendar it creates for itself, called "seva plan". google's permissions don't let it change any other event.
  • it doesn't access email, contacts, files, or anyone else's account.

where the information goes

  • seva runs on my own computer. its records stay there in a local database.
  • the google sign-in is kept in my computer's keychain.
  • seva's messages to me go through telegram.
  • when i chat with it, the text and the plan it needs to reply (including free/busy times, not other people's event details) are sent to anthropic's claude to write the reply.
  • nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to build profiles.

seva's use of information received from google apis follows the google api services user data policy, including the limited use requirements.

keeping or removing it

  • access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • deleting the "seva plan" calendar removes everything seva has written.
  • the local records are deleted when seva is uninstalled.
